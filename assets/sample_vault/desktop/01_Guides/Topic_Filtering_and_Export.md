# Topic Filtering & Roll Export

Capture ideas fluidly, tag key insights with directives, and aggregate them instantly across entire folders.

## Topic Filtering

1. **Tag Directives**: Use `@@imp`, `@@info`, `@@todo`, `@@review`, or custom tags anywhere in your notes.
2. **Left Sidebar TOPICS List**: Click any topic chip at the bottom of the left sidebar to filter the continuous roll to only show matching blocks.
3. **Copy & Export Tagged Roll**:
   - Click the **Copy Tagged Roll** button in the top bar to copy all filtered blocks to your clipboard as clean Markdown.
   - Click **Export Tagged Roll** to save the aggregated highlights into a new note file.
