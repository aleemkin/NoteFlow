# Continuous Reading Workflow

Traditional note tools force you to juggle dozens of open tabs. noteflow replaces tab sprawl with a seamless continuous reading stream.

## Zero Tab Fatigue

1. **Folder-Level Unification**: Selecting any folder aggregates every note and drawing into a single vertical scroll stream.
2. **Deep-Link Scrolling**: Click any file in the left sidebar tree or any heading in the right outline to smoothly scroll directly to that point.
3. **Embedded Drawing Previews**: Drawings display with high-resolution vector snapshots without disrupting your reading flow. Double-click or click the open icon to edit.
