# Continuous Reading on Mobile

Mobile screens are too compact for dozens of open tabs. noteflow aggregates notes into one smooth, distraction-free reading stream.

## Zero Tab Hassle

1. **Folder Roll Stream**: All notes in the active directory stream seamlessly one after the other.
2. **Jump with Outline**: Tap the outline icon in the top right to open the section drawer and jump immediately to any topic.
3. **Instant Tag Filtering**: Tap tag chips (`@@imp`, `@@todo`) in the drawer to isolate key sections in seconds.
