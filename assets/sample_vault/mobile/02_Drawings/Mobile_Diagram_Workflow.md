# Mobile Diagram Workflow

Sketch ideas freely with your finger or stylus directly inside noteflow Mobile.

## The Canvas Tab

Tap **Canvas** in the bottom navigation bar to open the Excalidraw vector workspace:

1. **Draw Shapes**: Rectangles, ellipses, diamond flowchart blocks, and freehand drawing.
2. **Connect with Smart Arrows**: Connect elements with auto-binding arrows that stick when shapes move.
3. **Pinch & Pan**: Use two fingers to zoom in for detailed annotations or pan across the infinite canvas.
4. **Instant Auto-Save**: Drawings save automatically as `.excalidraw` and rasterized `.excalidraw.png` previews.
