# Welcome to noteflow 📱

noteflow Mobile is your touch-first, local-first knowledge notebook designed for seamless reading, quick note capture, and finger-friendly vector sketching.

## 4 Core Mobile Tabs

Explore the bottom navigation bar to switch between the 4 dedicated views:

- 📝 **Notes**: Continuous vertical notebook stream of all notes and diagrams in the active folder. Read without tab clutter.
- ✏️ **Editor**: Distraction-free Markdown writing. Swipe horizontally between the raw editor and live formatted preview!
- 🎨 **Canvas**: Standalone Excalidraw vector sketchbook. Sketch diagrams, flowcharts, or wireframes with touch or stylus.
- 💼 **Vault**: Workspace metrics, quick workspace switching, and local storage management.

## Mobile Navigation Drawers

- **Left Drawer (☰)**: Tap the hamburger menu in the top left to open your Folder Tree and manage files.
- **Right Drawer**: Tap the outline icon in the top right to view the Document Outline, jump to any section, or filter by topic tags (`@@imp`, `@@info`, `@@todo`).
