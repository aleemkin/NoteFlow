# Document Outline & Section Reordering

The right sidebar provides a unified Document Outline inspector for exploring and organizing your notes.

## Section Filtering

Use the search bar at the top of the Document Outline panel (`Filter sections...`) to quickly filter sections across all notes in the active folder.

## Drag-and-Drop Reordering

1. Click the **Reorder** icon in the outline header to enter reorder mode.
2. Drag and drop any section or file tile to rearrange the sequence.
3. Click **Done** — the order is immediately saved to disk in `.kn/sequences.json`.
