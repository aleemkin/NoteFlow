# Continuous Reading Experience

In traditional note apps, navigating a project requires jumping across dozens of disconnected tabs.

## Zero Tab Clutter

noteflow treats each folder as a unified, continuous reading stream:

1. **Folder-Level Cohesion**: Opening any folder rolls all contained documents and diagrams into a single, cohesive vertical view.
2. **Smooth Scrolling**: Navigate smoothly through your notebook without losing reading context.
3. **Deep Linking**: Click any section in the Document Outline inspector on the right to jump directly to that heading or drawing.

@@info #mark_guide_01 title="Contextual Reading"
Clicking any file in the left sidebar smoothly scrolls to that note's exact location in the active folder roll.
@@/info
